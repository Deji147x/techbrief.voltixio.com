Our Mission

Tech Brief was built on a simple belief: technology news should be fast, original, and accessible to everyone. We built an automated pipeline that pulls from 40+ premium RSS sources, rewrites every article using a locally-hosted large language model, and publishes the result — every single hour, around the clock.

No paywalls. No copy-paste journalism. No tracking pixels selling your data. Just clean, AI-rewritten coverage of the stories shaping the world of technology.

We cover the six verticals that matter most: AI & Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, Startups & VC, Big Tech, Gadgets & Hardware, and Space & Science.

🚀

Publish
